1 Reference voice

10–15 seconds of clean speech is ideal. Longer costs generation time on every request without improving the clone. Any format your device produces works — it is converted in the browser.

This must be the words in the clip above — not the text you want generated. A mismatch is the single most common cause of a clone that sounds right but says the wrong thing.

    2 Consent

    Cloning a voice requires the speaker's spoken consent. The service issues a sentence containing their name, today's date and two random words; recording it proves the consent was given now, for this voice.

    4 Speak
